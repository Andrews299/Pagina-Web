  • Rush:They pass by your room. The two ways to avoid them are by hiding in a closet/bed or in a blind spot.
  • Ambush:It passes through your room but bounces off 2 to 3 times. The two ways to avoid it are by hiding in a closet/bed or in a blind spot.

    The 5 most used items in Minecraft are fundamental elements for survival, mobility, and progress in the game.
  1. Pickaxe: This is the most important tool in the game, essential for mining stone, coal, iron, and diamonds. From the initial wooden pickaxe to the netherite pickaxe, you'll use it constantly.
  2. Building Blocks (Wood and Stone): These are the foundation of everything in the game. Generic wood or cobblestone blocks are used to create shelters and bridges, as well as to build redstone mechanisms.
  3. Torches: These are vital for illuminating dark areas and preventing hostile mobs from appearing in your buildings or mining areas.
  4. Sword: The primary weapon for defending against the dangers of the world. Diamond or netherite swords are essential in any confrontation.
  5. Food: Vital for keeping the hunger bar full and regenerating health. Players often prioritize high-quality foods like steaks or golden carrots.
